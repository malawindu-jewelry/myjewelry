Pricing

Metal prices

Silver, gold and copper follow the live market (the same prices as your Metal Prices widget). Brass and bronze have no live price, so enter what you pay. Untick "Live" to type your own price instead.

Match your supplier's prices (Rio Grande)

The market price is raw metal only. Suppliers charge extra to make it into wire. Look up a wire's price per foot on riogrande.com and enter it once. The calculator works out their extra charge and keeps it while the metal price follows the market. Gauges you haven't added use the closest one you have.
